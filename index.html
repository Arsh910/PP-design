Our Story

“Everyone Said I’d Quit in Two Months.”

Pretty Petals began in 2021, when Bisman was 19 and pouring raw resin kits in her bedroom. The first pieces were messy and far from perfect, but the curiosity never faded.

Five years of late nights later, she preserves wedding garlands, invitation frames and the milestones people can’t bear to lose. What everyone called a passing phase now has a permanent home: our own studio, workshop and showroom at CCC, Zirakpur.

Bisman, founder

See all FAQs
How do I place an order?

Add pieces to your cart and check out, or send us a DM on Instagram or a message on WhatsApp with the piece you like.

Do you deliver outside Zirakpur?

Yes, across India. Most orders arrive in 5–8 days.

How much is delivery?

Free on orders over ₹2,499. Below that, a flat fee is shown at checkout.

How long does a custom piece take?

7–10 days after you approve the design preview.

Can I send my own flowers?

Yes. Dried flowers work best. We will guide you on how to send them.

Do you gift wrap?

Yes, free, with a handwritten note if you add one at checkout.
