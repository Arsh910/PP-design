Frequently Asked Questions

Ask us anything
How do I place an order?

Send us a DM on Instagram or a message on WhatsApp with the piece you like. We will confirm availability, price and delivery with you.

Can I order a custom resin piece?

Yes. Share a name, a photo, a colour or a date that matters. We confirm the size, colours and details with you before we pour, then finish and pack it ready to keep or to gift.

Do you make festive hampers?

Yes. The Floral, Pink Frame, Wooden and Woven hampers are made on enquiry. DM us to choose yours.

Do you deliver outside Zirakpur?

Message us with your location and we will let you know the delivery options for your order.
